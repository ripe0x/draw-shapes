Mix is the launch table: 38 single Shapes at 0.01, 28 packs of two, 19 packs of three, 10 at 0.05, 4 at 0.1, 1 at 0.5. Items list at backing plus the 700 bps split. Major GRAIL takes 80 percent of the fee in 0.5 ETH steps and lists at 75 percent of backing, minor takes 20 percent in 0.1 ETH steps up to 1 ETH and lists at face. Ticket is the harmonic mean of list prices times 1.05. Odds per item are 1 over its list price, which is why the GRAIL is rare.

Fee times deposits is the GRAIL seed. NAV after the fee funds the board. Yield per day is premium times tickets per day over NAV. After the window the pool is assumed to fill to the volume cap on day one, so yield drops to the cap rate.

The window premium is set by the admin with setFeeSplit at open and reverted when the window closes. The feed takes the difference, so list prices and tickets are unchanged.
