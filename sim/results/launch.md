# Single-sided launch model

Generated by `sim/launch.py` (deterministic). Assumptions in the file header; these are model results, not
forecasts. FDV is in ETH at the pool price (1B supply). "Exit" is what selling the position into the pool at
that moment would return after the 3% fee.

the price can at worst return to the start. Without conversions the only genesis buying is the 3% buyback, and
full farm selling takes the price back to (or near) the start: opening buyers then exit with less than they
paid, the farm having taken their ETH. That is the cost of the 19% farm under a single-sided launch with buys
closed during genesis. Conversions are what keep buying in the pool; the 2% bonus is there to encourage them.

## 4. Single-sided against a small seeded two-sided position

Same start price (10 ETH FDV), 1 ETH of opening buys, 20 ETH of genesis tickets, no conversions, farmers sell
everything: the case in which sells outrun buys and the floor binds. The seeded variant adds a full-range
position with the owner's ETH at the start price.

| launch | owner ETH paired | lowest possible FDV | FDV at end | pool ETH at end | farm ETH out | of which the owner's |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single-sided (adopted) | 0 ETH | the start (10 ETH) | 10.02 ETH | 0.00 ETH | 1.51 ETH | 0.00 ETH |
| seeded 1 ETH | 1 ETH | none: below the start | 6.51 ETH | 0.81 ETH | 1.69 ETH | 0.19 ETH |
| seeded 5 ETH | 5 ETH | none: below the start | 9.04 ETH | 4.75 ETH | 1.75 ETH | 0.24 ETH |

Reading: seeding ETH gives farm sellers the owner's ETH as well and removes the price floor; it does not protect
opening buyers. The single-sided launch needs no ETH and its floor is the start price.

## 5. Recommendation

- **Start at 10 ETH FDV (`START_TICK` 184,200, ≈ 99.8M ENGINE per ETH).** 1 ETH of opening buys moves the price
  by about a quarter (5 ETH FDV: more than half), and a 5 ETH opening leaves the largest buyer about 14% of
  supply (5 ETH FDV: 24%). 5 ETH FDV is the alternative if the owner prefers to give opening buyers more upside
  and the farm less ETH value (0.95 ETH at the start price); it is better for opening buyers in the worst case
  (section 3) at the price of more concentration. 20 ETH and above are worse for opening buyers in most cases.
- **The farm can take the opening market's ETH.** The rate cap pays 10% of ticket ETH valued at the start price;
  ENGINE trades above the start once anyone buys, so the rewards are worth more, and with public buys closed
  during genesis farm sales meet only the opening market's ETH, conversions and buybacks. In the worst case
  (no conversions, a thin opening market) opening buyers lose most of what they paid. Options, none applied
  without the owner's approval: a smaller farm (for example 10% with 9% more to the float); valuing the rate
  cap at a time-weighted pool price instead of the start price (a contract change); or a lower start FDV.
- **No seeded ETH.** It does not protect buyers and puts the owner's ETH up as exit liquidity.
