## 3. Reading and recommendation

- Packs cost one mint fee per Shape inside, so a Pack of several Shapes costs more to list than a Shape of the
  same backing; that shows in the mint-fee share and lowers the return slightly. Packs fill the values the
  Shapes ladder skips (0.03-0.04, 0.06-0.09, 0.15-0.45 ETH), so a board can step more gently from the common
  prize to the headline.
- The Shapes board stays the launch default (owner's decision). Board A has seven prize levels, a slightly lower
  ticket and about the same margin per draw, and needs half the board capital (7.72 against 15.15 ETH); its
  0.5 ETH headline is rarer (1 in 393 against 1 in 73) because only 5 rows carry it. Board B has a 1 ETH headline
  (1 in 715) and more mid prizes at a slightly higher ticket and margin, on 12 ETH of board. Either is
  `BoardPolicy.setBoard` by governance; rows change as they are next vacated, and Pack rows must be at least
  Shape Packs' 0.03 ETH minimum.
