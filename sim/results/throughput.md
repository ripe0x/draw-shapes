# Purchase gate throughput

Generated by `sim/throughput.py` (fixed seeds). Assumptions in the file header; model results, not forecasts.
Each cell: 3 runs of 4 days. `completed` is draws that settled with a prize per day; `rejected` is the share of
draws refused before any fee (gate or FWA's VRF cap); `refunded` is the share of accepted draws FWA refunded on
drift (the VRF fee is spent); VRF cost is per completed draw at 1 gwei, refunds included.

## Reading

- Rejections are refusals before payment, not losses. At limits 3-5 most come from the in-flight limit itself:
  one batch of 5 fills it, so the next purchase within the minute or so a word takes is refused. At limit 10
  two batches fit, and at 10 ETH a day almost nothing is refused; at 50 ETH a day the rest come from the board
  falling toward the floor between restocks.
- Refunds come from FWA's drift guard: each cheap prize captured raises the live fee, and a request settling
  after several captures can find the fee more than 10% above its escrow. More in flight means more captures
  between a request's quote and its settlement, so refunds rise with the limit, and fall with board size.
- Launch value: **limit 10 on the 100-row board**: the most completed draws; refunds stay small. On a board cut
  to about 50 rows, lower the limit (governance `setCapital`).
