Reading: Shapes leads FWA on the cash-out lens at the floor (0.5 point), trails it by 2.8 points at the top rate, and
trails at face value by 9.0 to 12.3 points because a Shapes ticket carries FWA's protocol half of the edge (2.2%), the
mint line (1.4%), the keeper line (1.5%), the depositor premium, the operator line (2.0%), maintenance and the GRAIL
feed (4.5%, returned to players as prizes). Shapes leads on the floor (zero below-half sessions) and on ETH
redeemability. The premium averages 2.4% at 3,355 pulls a day, so the volume-weighted return there is 85.8%.
