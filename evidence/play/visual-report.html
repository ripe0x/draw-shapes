One week of play at 500 tickets a day

3,413 tickets, 50 ETH opening cohort, launch parameters from script/env/mainnet.env, played against the deployed Shapes and FWA V2 contracts. Compared with FWA's V2 main pool, from its full 4,090-listing board on 30 September 2026. Tickets on both sides exclude VRF and gas. Every figure comes from evidence/play/ CSVs committed on the branch.

Two things to look at first. Replacement mint fees take half of the pool's gross margin at this ticket size, because the mainnet Shapes mint fee is 0.001 ETH per Shape and the median capture is a 0.01 ETH Shape. And the board relists about every 41 minutes, so FWA's 5 minute purchase pause covers 14 percent of the day.

Depositors

Depositors are the house. The opening cohort paid 50 ETH; after the entry contribution to the GRAIL buckets (8.6 ETH) and the first board's mint fees, their shares were worth 41.2 ETH, so NAV per share started at 0.996 and a share breaks even for the cohort at 1.21. The week ended at 1.035, up 3.9 percent, which at that pace reaches break-even in about five more weeks. FWA's V2 main pool has no depositor side: FWA supplies the inventory and keeps the 5 percent surcharge plus the 10 percent settlement discount.

Where each ticket goes

Per 1 ETH of tickets, derived from the recorded tickets and captures and the fee split in SPEC 9. VRF is zero at the fork block and is paid by the player on top on mainnet.

Board and pauses

Each inventory change restarts FWA's 300 second purchase pause. The Weight trigger fired 246 times in the week; one capture of a 0.5 ETH Shape removes 10 percent of the board's weight on its own.

GRAIL

Two tracks fed by the entry contribution and the feed share. The minor track reached 0.6 ETH of backing and was captured twice, the first time at hour 80. Odds are the listing's share of FWA selection weight at that hour.

Method and caveats

Runner: test/scenario/PlayMonth.t.sol, deterministic for seed 1, VRF fulfilled by impersonating the coordinator, 20 rotating players buying 1 to 5 tickets, rotate called whenever rotateDue() is not None. Report script: script/report.py. The 2,000 a day run did not complete and is open. The revenue split is computed from the recorded tickets and captures with the SPEC 9 formula; the runner does not log the buckets directly. The FWA snapshot was read once with cast; the next version reads it inside a fork test so the Foundry RPC cache serves it.
