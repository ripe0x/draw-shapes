Method and caveats

Runner: test/scenario/PlayMonth.t.sol, deterministic for seed 1, VRF fulfilled by impersonating the coordinator, 20 rotating players buying 1 to 5 tickets, rotate and settle called whenever rotateDue() or settleDue() is not None. Report script: script/report.py --visual, which fills evidence/play/visual-report.template.html from the run CSVs and evidence/play/visual-static.json. The 2,000 a day run is produced on demand (docs/PLAY.md). Mint fees and the revenue split are computed from the recorded tickets and captures with the SPEC 9 formula. NAV figures are the pool's ordinary NAV, which excludes the GRAIL fund.
